# Code of Conduct

Dua follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and take feedback
graciously, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks,
and publishing someone's private information aren't acceptable anywhere in the
project: issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers at `[CONTACT EMAIL]`. Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers will review every report and may remove comments,
close threads, or ban someone from the project, temporarily or permanently.
